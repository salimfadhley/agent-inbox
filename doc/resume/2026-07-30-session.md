## Things a future session should know

**`spec-kitty plan` is interactive.** It prompts on stdin and waits. It is not hung. Pipe
answers in. This cost ten minutes before it was understood.

**`spec-commit` refuses this repo** — insists it is on `main` even when checked out on the
mission branch. Plans and specs have been committed with plain `git` instead, so two
missions sit outside spec-kitty's ledger.

**Issue #25 — the suite flakes about 1 in 3.** Federation servers leak binary into a later
test's capture; it blames an arbitrary test. **Re-run once before believing a red suite.**

**Portainer deploys are not idempotent** (#27, #32). Three deploys, three problems — twice
leaving the hub down while looking deployed. `ship.sh` now starts stragglers before
verifying.
